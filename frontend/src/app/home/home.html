Features

Match History

Look up a summoner and browse their recent matches across draft and ranked queues.

Match Timeline

See every relevant event (e.g., kills or objectives) in chronological order.

Scoreboard Replay

Scrub through the match and see gold, level, and items at different moments.

FUTURE FUNCTIONALITIES

Win Probability

A dynamic graph based on gold, XP, and objective control.

Event Impact

Contextual evaluation of how each kill or objective swung the game.

What-If Simulation

Undo a relevant event and see how the evaluation changes.
